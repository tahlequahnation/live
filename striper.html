Striper Adventure

Striper Catch, Clean, Cook

Guided by Capt. Wayne • 6 hour excursion • $489 • Up to 4 people

What to expect

Out here, it’s just you, the rod, and the raw pull of a striper that doesn’t want to come aboard. No gimmicks—just the kind of fishing that gets your blood pumping and your grip tightening. Capt. Wayne will put you right where the big ones are, show you the tactics that work, and keep the action coming. By day’s end, we’ll haul in your catch, clean it up, and throw it over the fire alongside hearty, local seasonal sides. Cold drinks, hot grill, and the satisfaction of knowing you earned every bite. This isn’t just a day on the water—it’s a man’s day well spent.
